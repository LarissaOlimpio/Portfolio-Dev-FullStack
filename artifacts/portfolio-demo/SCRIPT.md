# Larissa Olimpio — portfolio film

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Meet a full-stack developer whose work connects business rules, thoughtful engineering, and useful interfaces.
- **Identity:** The source portfolio's midnight navy, electric periwinkle, and warm yellow; Plus Jakarta Sans with DM Mono accents.
- **Hero:** Larissa's illustrated portrait and the `larissa.dev` identity, followed by real project previews.
- **Motion:** Hand-drawn ellipse reveals, compound push-throughs on the portrait and project screens, and fast editorial typography.
- **Arc:** Personal introduction → range of experience → evidence in projects → a human, resolved contact close.
- **Payoff:** The final portrait crop reduces into a small signature beside a concise invitation, unlike the opening's oversized image.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, 30 seconds.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–5.00 s / 5.00 s — “Software that works.”
**Purpose:** Establish a real person and the portfolio's central promise immediately.

**Exact content:** `LARISSA OLIMPIO` at 0.25 s; `larissa.dev` at 0.65 s; `I turn business rules into software that works.` at 1.50 s; `FULL STACK · JAVA · REACT` at 3.20 s. Readable through the outgoing move.

**Composition:** Midnight navy field. The supplied illustrated portrait fills the right half at ~73% of frame height, tilted slightly in a cobalt frame with a yellow offset backing. Large type occupies the left third; 5% safe inset.

**Artistic idea:** The portrait’s cobalt frame and offset yellow backing create a crisp graphic silhouette against the deep navy, while the underline beneath “works” gives the copy its only yellow mark.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.20: portrait and name arrive as a sharp paired snap. 0.30–0.75: brand label locks in; yellow backing panel settles behind the portrait. 0.90–1.45: headline reveals on two beats. 1.60–2.70: portrait makes a subtle scale settle. 2.75–3.35: skill line types in. 3.75–5.00: portrait pushes through the lens; keep the face unobstructed until the next shot arrives.

**Handoff:** The portrait frame scales through the lens while the headline shears away; incoming skills pills appear inside the outgoing cobalt rectangle as it opens into their field.

**Assets/technique:** Supplied portrait `public/images/larissa-portrait.jpg`; CSS planes, SVG ellipse; Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental groove; cut at 5 s lands on a generated rhythmic accent.

### Shot 02 / 5.00–11.00 s / 6.00 s — “A toolkit with range.”
**Purpose:** Demonstrate both the human profile and actual technical breadth.

**Exact content:** `ABOUT / SOBRE` at 5.10 s; `Build. Learn. Share.` at 5.35 s; pills `Java`, `Spring Boot`, `React`, `TypeScript`, `NestJS`, `PostgreSQL`, `Oracle`, `Datadog`, `Docker`, `SQL` introduced in short groups from 6.10–8.80 s; `Engineering, with curiosity.` at 9.30 s.

**Composition:** Deep indigo, a sweeping horizontal ribbon of pills across a 3D-ish perspective grid; giant outlined `BUILD` sits cropped behind the pills. Left title zone, center skills, lower-right statement.

**Artistic idea:** Skills orbit the ellipse left by the portrait, forming a readable, organized constellation rather than a static tag cloud.

**Choreography:** 5.00–5.45: ring iris opens; `ABOUT / SOBRE` lands. 5.60–6.40: `BUILD` wipes on as an outlined word. 6.10–8.80: three rows of pills snap into orbit in alternating directions; highlights pulse yellow only on Java, Spring Boot, React. 8.90–9.70: camera pulls back to reveal full constellation, supporting line resolves. 10.00–11.00: pills collapse into a cobalt rectangle; the rectangle hinges away as the next scene's panel.

**Handoff:** The cobalt panel remains at the same location, hinges open with perspective rotation, and reveals experience cards. Compound panel fold + pill-stream scale + continuous carrier.

**Assets/technique:** CSS/SVG pills and grid; no additional images.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental continues; cut at 11 s lands on a generated rhythmic accent.

### Shot 03 / 11.00–17.00 s / 6.00 s — “From production systems to planning data.”
**Purpose:** Ground the portfolio in real experience instead of abstract claims.

**Exact content:** `EXPERIENCE` at 11.05 s; `Embraer` / `Planning data · Oracle · Highcharts` at 11.50 s; `Alura` / `Java · Spring Boot · production systems` at 13.15 s; `Supliu` / `React · TypeScript · data interfaces` at 14.60 s; `From requirements to release.` at 15.70 s.

**Composition:** Cobalt-blue field. Three asymmetrical white-edged timeline panels form a rising diagonal, with one active yellow company label at a time. Type stays large and restrained.

**Artistic idea:** A single yellow plotted line connects the three company panels like a trajectory, then resolves into a browser chrome line.

**Choreography:** 11.00–11.55: panel unfolds and first company snaps forward. 11.65–12.70: yellow path draws from company mark to a stylized scatter plot. 12.90–13.60: camera whip to Alura panel; copy replaces, path continues. 14.00–14.80: Supliu panel rises from below; supporting copy lands. 15.00–15.80: zoom out reveals the complete connected path. 16.00–17.00: path pushes into lens and becomes the browser address bar of the next shot.

**Handoff:** Yellow path exits at same angle/position as the address bar enters. Combine 3D panel fold, chart-path draw, and blur-slice copy replacement.

**Assets/technique:** Facts from source experience data; authored graphic panels, no logos beyond plain text company names.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental continues; cut at 17 s lands on a generated rhythmic accent.

### Shot 04 / 17.00–24.00 s / 7.00 s — “Projects you can explore.”
**Purpose:** Showcase the actual work and visual assets in the portfolio.

**Exact content:** `SELECTED WORK` at 17.05 s; `Workout Tracker — Train Up` at 17.55 s; `Full-stack · React · NestJS · PostgreSQL` at 18.25 s; `Digital Retail BI` at 20.05 s; `SQL → Power BI` at 20.60 s; `Events API` at 22.05 s; `Java · Spring Boot · Security` at 22.55 s.

**Composition:** First a large browser-window mockup showing the supplied Workout Tracker poster, nearly full height and angled just a few degrees. It pivots to a clean dashboard image occupying the left 65%, with a right-side title stack; ends on a dark browser frame.

**Artistic idea:** The portfolio's real project images are not generic cards: each browser window fills the lens, and a graph line is traced over the dashboard's own plotted geometry.

**Choreography:** 17.00–17.55: address bar expands into browser frame. 17.65–19.50: Workout Tracker poster reveals via split aperture and holds at a fixed crop; title and stack of authentic project technologies arrive sequentially. 19.65–20.15: a flat split reveal replaces it with the data analytics still. 20.35–21.55: three chart strokes draw in succession; `Digital Retail BI` and pipeline labels appear. 21.70–22.35: 160ms hard cut to Events API project panel. 22.50–23.20: project tags pop; 23.30–24.00: frame folds to a small yellow square, carried into final scene.

**Handoff:** Browser frame folds into an inset square, then unfolds as the end-card's signature bracket. Compound push-through + chart trace + fold.

**Assets/technique:** Copied, actual source assets `public/images/workout-poster.jpg` and `public/images/data-poster.jpg`; no fabricated product claims.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental continues; cut at 24 s lands on a generated rhythmic accent.

### Shot 05 / 24.00–30.00 s / 6.00 s — “Thoughtful engineering. Open conversations.”
**Purpose:** Close with values and the portfolio's contact invitation, not another feature list.

**Exact content:** `ENGINEERING WITH CARE` at 24.15 s; `Clean architecture.` at 24.70 s; `Critical use of AI.` at 25.55 s; `Always learning.` at 26.35 s; `PORTUGUÊS  /  ENGLISH` at 27.15 s; `Have a project in mind?` at 27.90 s; `Larissa Olimpio · São José dos Campos, Brazil` at 28.50 s; `larissa.dev` at 29.20 s.

**Composition:** Palette inverts to soft periwinkle-grey, with portrait in a compact circular crop on the left; open negative space carries the value statements and final copy on the right. Yellow line closes around the identity.

**Artistic idea:** The opening's huge portrait becomes a small human signature; the closing ellipse is drawn as a single continuous line and leaves a tiny gap precisely where the opening ellipse resumes.

**Choreography:** 24.00–24.55: folded square unfolds into a portrait aperture, background inverts. 24.70–26.90: three value lines arrive one at a time with a small yellow underline that travels downward. 27.15–27.75: bilingual label splits into PT / EN, then recombines. 27.90–28.80: portrait settles, contact invitation and location resolve. 29.00–29.65: `larissa.dev` locks in; ellipse traces around the brand. 29.65–30.00: ellipse opens at its original start point; the brand word's dot becomes the opener's first yellow point for a seamless loop.

**Handoff:** Palette inversion + aperture expansion + physical continuity of the yellow ellipse; final dot match-cuts to shot 01's first mark.

**Assets/technique:** Supplied portrait asset; CSS ellipse and masked typography.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental resolves on final lockup; no voiceover or effects requested. Final accent at 29 s supports the lockup.
